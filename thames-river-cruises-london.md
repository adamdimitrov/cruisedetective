## Quick Verdict & Key Takeaways

1. **#1 Top Pick — Overall Best Sightseeing Cruise**: **Thames River Sightseeing (TRS)**
   - *Price*: From £15
   - *Departure Piers*: Westminster Pier, Embankment Pier, Festival Pier, Tower Bridge Quay, Greenwich Pier
   - *Why It Wins*: Unrivaled live audio commentary delivered with classic British humor by seasoned watermen, combined with open-air rooftop decks and heated lower cabins.

2. **#2 Best Modern Circular Sightseeing**: **London Eye River Cruise**
   - *Price*: From £16
   - *Pier*: London Eye Pier (Waterloo)
   - *Duration*: 40 minutes circular loop
   - *Why*: Direct departure under the London Eye wheel, excellent commentary, perfect add-on to South Bank sightseeing.

3. **#3 Best High-Adrenaline Thrill**: **Thames Rockets Speedboat Tour**
   - *Price*: From £55
   - *Pier*: London Eye Pier / Bankside
   - *Why*: 630hp twin-engine RIB reaching 35 knots past Canary Wharf with high-energy soundtrack.
