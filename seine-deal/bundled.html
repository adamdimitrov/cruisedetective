Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to select a specific time slot? ▾

No. Your ticket is a flexible day pass valid for any scheduled departure on your selected travel date. Boats depart every 30 to 60 minutes between 10:00 and 22:30, so you can arrive whenever it best fits your sightseeing itinerary.

Where exactly does the cruise depart from? ▾

Boarding takes place at Port de la Bourdonnais, Pier 3 (Ponton 3), located directly at the foot of the Eiffel Tower in the 7th arrondissement of Paris. Simply walk down the pedestrian ramp from the riverbank to the dock.

How do I skip the ticket line? ▾

When you reach the dock, completely bypass the box office ticket lines. Head directly to the boarding gates at Pier 3 and present the digital barcode on your smartphone screen to be scanned for direct entry.

Can I cancel or reschedule if my plans change? ▾

Yes. Bookings made through Tiqets include a 100% full refund policy if canceled up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled visit date.

Do children require tickets? ▾

Infants and toddlers aged 0 to 3 travel free of charge and do not need a ticket. Children aged 4 to 11 require a reduced child ticket (€9.50). All guests aged 12 and older require standard adult admission (€18).

What is the best time of day to take the cruise? ▾

Sunset and early evening departures are the most popular. A sunset cruise lets you admire the Parisian architecture during the golden hour, while evening cruises offer an unobstructed vantage point on the water to watch the Eiffel Tower sparkle after dusk (sparkling occurs for 5 minutes at the beginning of every hour after dark).
