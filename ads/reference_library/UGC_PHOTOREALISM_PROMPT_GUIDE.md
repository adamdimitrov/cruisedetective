# UGC & Photorealism Prompt Engineering Guide (Anti-AI Airbrushing Formula)

## 1. The Anatomy of Why Native AI Prompts Look Synthetic
When prompting multimodal image models (Gemini Flash Image / Imagen 4), models default to the average of corporate commercial stock libraries:
1. **Skin**: Overly smoothed porcelain/plastic rendering with zero visible pore structures, zero micro-blemishes, and zero subsurface scattering variance.
2. **Lighting**: Studio-balanced 3-point rim lighting that defies real-world physics (both the subject's face and the dark night river are unnaturally well-lit).
3. **Posing & Expressions**: Symmetrical "catalog model" teeth-baring grins directly facing the camera with rigid posture.
4. **Environment**: Sterile, pristine environments without real-world clutter or camera artifacts.

---

## 2. The 5-Pillar UGC Photorealism Formula

To force the model into authentic, raw smartphone UGC (User-Generated Content), every prompt must systematically inject these 5 optical anchors:

```text
[1. CAMERA SENSOR & OPTICS]
Candid handheld eye-level snapshot taken on an iPhone 15 Pro, 24mm f/1.8 equivalent lens. Authentic low-light sensor noise / ISO 1200 grain. Unretouched smartphone color science, dynamic range with naturally deep shadows and bright specular highlights from candles and Paris streetlamps, slight optical lens flare on the glass.

[2. HUMAN ANATOMY & CASUAL STYLING]
Real everyday travelers in their early 30s. Completely unretouched, natural skin textures with visible pores, fine lines around the eyes, natural lip texture, and subtle skin warmth. Candid mid-laugh or subtle relaxed smile—NOT an over-rehearsed stock photo pose. Natural, slightly imperfect hair with stray strands. Authentic clothing with real fabric wrinkles and weight (e.g., unlined linen jacket, textured knit sweater, simple matte black dress).

[3. ENVIRONMENTAL CLUTTER & TEXTURE]
Lived-in candid restaurant table physics: crusty French baguette in a wire basket, real butter smear on bread plate, condensation droplets on chilled white wine glass, flickering votive candle with melting wax, real reflections of Paris monuments in the curved glass window.

[4. VENUE ARCHITECTURAL ACCURACY]
Exact Bateaux Mouches ship geometry: enclosed panoramic glass salon with dark wood acoustic ceiling embedded with warm pinpoint star lights, central fixed acoustic performance stage with grand piano and violinist, OR wide open-air 360° upper rooftop deck upstairs (NEVER fictitious exterior side gangways).

[5. NEGATIVE GUARDRAILS (IN POSITIVE PHRASING)]
Completely avoid porcelain airbrushed skin, CGI rendering, 3D smooth plastic faces, studio beauty lighting, symmetrical stock photo poses, corporate brochure models, matching tourist costumes, and green corner bookmark icons.
```
