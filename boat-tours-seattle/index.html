The 8 Best Seattle Boat Tours and Whale Watching Trips (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Owner & Head Cruise Detective
Independent side-by-side comparison
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

Seattle's boat tours each do a different job, so pick by what you want to see: the skyline and the port in 1 hour (€39), the Ballard Locks lifting your boat between salt and fresh water (2 hours, one way, €51), a sunset sail on a schooner (€73), or orcas and humpbacks on a 3.5-hour trip from Anacortes, 90 minutes north. The Locks cruise ends on the other side of town; plan the trip back.

I compared the Seattle boat tours you can book online and kept the 8 that are genuinely different, on what decides it: the route, the length, the boat, narration or not, the pier and the price, checked on each seller's own page.

Is a Seattle Boat Tour Worth It?

Yes. Seattle sits on Elliott Bay with mountains on both sides, and the water is the only place to see the skyline, Mount Rainier and the Olympics together. Start with the 1-hour harbor cruise (€39); add the Locks cruise if you like engineering and houseboats, or a sunset sail for the evening.

For whales you need to head north to the San Juan Islands (#4).

More boat trips compared side by side: San Francisco Bay cruises, San Diego whale watching and harbor cruises, Chicago architecture boat tours and Boston boat tours.

Where Seattle Boat Tours Leave From

The harbor, Locks and sunset cruises leave from the downtown waterfront: Pier 55 (Argosy) and Pier 66 (the schooner). The Locks cruise also runs from South Lake Union. Whale watching starts in Anacortes.

Best Time for a Seattle Boat Tour

The harbor cruise runs all year. June to September is the dry season with long sunsets around 8 to 9 pm; whale watching runs March to November. Bring a layer at any time: the Sound is cool even in summer.

Seattle Boat Tours: FAQ

How much is a boat tour in Seattle?

€39 for the 1-hour harbor cruise, €46 to €73 for sunset cruises, €51 for the Locks cruise and €109 for orca watching.

What is the best boat tour in Seattle?

The 1-hour Argosy harbor cruise (4.8). For something unique, the Locks cruise through the Ballard Locks.

Can you see orcas from Seattle?

Not reliably from downtown; the best trips leave from Anacortes or the San Juan Islands, March to November.

Is the Locks cruise round trip?

No, it's one way between the Waterfront and Lake Union.

Where do Seattle boat tours leave from?

Pier 55 and Pier 66 on the downtown waterfront.

When is the best time?

June to September for dry weather and sunsets.
