The 10 Best Vistula River Cruises in Warsaw (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Owner & Head Cruise Detective
Independent side-by-side comparison
Last updated: Nov 19, 2026

The right Vistula cruise is the one that fits your day: a cheap hour past the Old Town on a traditional wooden boat, a heated cruise that still sails in winter, a sunset or a drink on the river, or a private boat, a speedboat or a wildlife trip. This page gets you to that boat in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared the Vistula cruises you can book online and kept the 10 that each do one job best, on what decides it: whether it sails in winter, the boat, the group size, what is included, the pier and the price, checked on each seller's own page. Summary & Review Overview ▾

This is the cruise most visitors to Warsaw end up on, and the numbers explain why: 2,320 reviews with a 4.5 average, the lowest price of any cruise that still sails in winter, and departures every day of the year except the Christmas week.

You board one of eight 12-seat wooden galars, replicas of the flat-bottomed boats that once carried salt and grain down the Vistula, at the big brown two-level barge called Marina Warszawa below the Copernicus Science Centre. The 54-minute loop runs past the Old Town and the Royal Castle, the Warsaw Mermaid, the Praga port and the National Stadium, with the wild green right bank on the other side.

Commentary comes from an audio guide in English and 7 other languages on your phone. Reviewers keep repeating the same tip: bring earphones. The boats are heated in the cold months and there are blankets on board.

💡 Tip: Go for the last departure before sunset. Reviewers who sailed at golden hour describe the lit-up Old Town on the way back as the highlight.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Same kind of boat as #1, a traditional wooden galar, but a different evening: never more than 11 guests, a skipper who talks about Warsaw rather than an audio guide, and a welcome drink included. In spring and summer it is a glass of alcohol-free prosecco; from autumn it switches to mulled wine.

You board at the Dworzec Wodny barge in Powiśle and sail north past the Copernicus Science Centre, the Museum of Modern Art and the old Praga port towards the Old and New Towns, then back past the National Stadium, Poniatówka beach and a small wild island. The loop passes under 5 bridges.

The galar has an open deck and a covered deck, blankets for the evening chill and USB and 220V charging. You are welcome to bring your own bottle.

💡 Tip: The same operator sells a sunset version and a 35-minute version of this cruise. They are the same boat, so pick the time that suits you rather than the listing.

Is a Vistula River Cruise in Warsaw Worth It?

Yes, if you go in the right month on the right boat. For €16 to €23 and under an hour, you see the Old Town and Royal Castle from the river, which is the view on every Warsaw postcard, plus something few European capitals have: a wild, green right bank with sandbanks and riverside forest opposite the skyline.

Do not expect Paris. Warsaw's boats are small (9 to 12 guests on most of them), there are no big glass-roofed tour ships, and the commentary is either a skipper or an audio guide on your phone. That is the charm: you sit close to the water on a traditional wooden galar or a gondola, not on a 300-seat boat. Comparing cities? See how it stacks up against the best Seine cruises in Paris, the Thames cruises in London and the Amsterdam canal cruises.

Half of the cruises above board within a 5-minute walk of each other on the left-bank boulevards below the Copernicus Science Centre (metro M2: Centrum Nauki Kopernik): Krypa at the brown two-level Marina Warszawa barge, Warsaw by BOAT, the iBarka, Miami Wars and the Warsaw Boats private boat at the Dworzec Wodny barges, and Flota 511 a little south at Solec 8. Kurka Wodna is further south on Bulwar Flotylli Wiślanej, and Rejsowisko is at Poniatowski Bridge, opposite the National Stadium.

Best Time for a Vistula Cruise

May to September is the main season. Every operator sails, the beaches on the right bank are busy, and the sun sets late (around 9pm in June), so an evening cruise ends in daylight. Book the last departure before sunset for the Old Town lit up on the way back.

October to March is a different list. In mid-November only the Krypa galars, the iBarka, Flota 511, the Warsaw Boats private boat and the wildlife safari are bookable every day, Kurka Wodna and Miami Wars sail on some days, and Warsaw by BOAT stops after 6 December. Sunset comes around 3:30pm in December, so even an afternoon cruise is a night cruise. Pick a heated or covered boat: the iBarka or the Krypa galars.

Watch the water level in late summer. In dry spells the Vistula drops and some boats change route (Kurka Wodna heads south to Siekierki instead of the Old Town). Flota 511's shallow-draft boat is built for it.

Vistula River Cruises in Warsaw: FAQ

How much does a Vistula river cruise in Warsaw cost?

Shared cruises cost €15.89 to €31.77 per person for 30 to 60 minutes. The best value is the Krypa galar at €17.06 for 54 minutes. A whole private boat for up to 12 starts at €211.05, and the 3-hour wildlife safari from Modlin is €113.47 per person.

Do Vistula cruises run in winter?

Some do. The Krypa galars (heated), the iBarka (glazed and heated, with a toilet), Flota 511's covered boat and the Warsaw Boats private boat are bookable every day in November and December. Kurka Wodna sails on selected dates and Miami Wars on weekends. The AQUATICA gondola and Rejsowisko restart in March and April.

Where do Warsaw river cruises leave from?

Most leave from the left-bank boulevards in Powiśle, below the Copernicus Science Centre (metro M2: Centrum Nauki Kopernik): the Marina Warszawa barge, the Dworzec Wodny barges and Solec 8. Kurka Wodna is a little south on Bulwar Flotylli Wiślanej, Rejsowisko is at Poniatowski Bridge, and the wildlife safari starts in Modlin, outside the city.

Which Warsaw cruise includes a drink?

Warsaw by BOAT's evening galar includes a welcome drink (alcohol-free prosecco in summer, mulled wine in winter), and Kurka Wodna includes coffee, tea, soft drinks and snacks. Krypa sells an evening version with one drink from €27.30, and the iBarka has a bar on board.

Is there an English-speaking guide on Vistula cruises?

Flota 511 has an English-speaking skipper, and Warsaw by BOAT's skippers tell stories about the city. Krypa and AQUATICA use an audio guide on your own phone, so bring headphones. The wildlife safari guide speaks Polish.

Can I bring my own drinks on a Vistula boat?

Yes on Warsaw by BOAT's galar and on Rejsowisko's motorboat, where eating and drinking on board is allowed. On the private galar, bringing your own drinks costs a PLN 100 corkage fee, while Kurka Wodna's private catamaran allows your own food and drinks at no extra charge.
