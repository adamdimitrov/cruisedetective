2026 Split Boat Tour Comparison

The 10 Best Boat Tours from Split to Hvar, the Blue Cave and Blue Lagoon (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Owner & Head Cruise Detective
Independent side-by-side comparison
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right boat tour from Split is the one that fits your day: a full day by speedboat to the Blue Cave, Vis and Hvar, a half day swimming in the Blue Lagoon, or a big boat with lunch and unlimited wine. Budget for the extra most visitors miss: every Blue Cave tour adds a cave ticket of about €18 to €24, paid in cash on the day. This page gets you to that boat in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared the boat tours from Split you can book online and kept the 10 that each do one job best, on what decides it: the route (Blue Cave and five islands, or Blue Lagoon and Trogir), the length, the boat (speedboat or big cruise boat), the group size, what is included and the real total with extras, checked on each seller's own page. Is a Boat Tour from Split Worth It?

Yes. The islands off Split are the reason most people visit, and a boat is the only way to string several together in one day. A half day to the Blue Lagoon costs €60 to €70; a full day to the Blue Cave, Vis and Hvar €79 to €145, plus the cave ticket.

Decide on the day: half day and swimming (#2, #6, #7), full day with the Blue Cave (#1, #4, #5), or a slower big boat with lunch and wine (#3, #9).

More boat trips compared side by side: Malta boat trips, Venice gondola rides, Lake Como boat tours and Bosphorus cruises in Istanbul.

All of them leave from the Split waterfront: the Riva (Obala Hrvatskog narodnog preporoda), Obala Lazareta by the ferry port, Trumbićeva obala, or Prokurative square just behind the Riva.

Best Time for a Boat Tour from Split

The season runs roughly April to October. June to early September is warmest and busiest, with the highest Blue Cave ticket price (€24). May, June and late September are the sweet spot: warm water, fewer boats and €18 cave tickets in the shoulder months.

The Blue Cave can close in rough seas; tours then swap in another stop. Book early in July and August.

Split Boat Tours: FAQ

How much is a Blue Cave tour from Split?

From €79 to €145 for the boat, plus the Blue Cave ticket: about €18 in shoulder season and €24 in summer, paid in cash.

What is the best boat tour from Split?

For the Blue Cave, Speed Line's 5-island day (4.9 from 1,752, €79). For a half day, the Blue Lagoon speedboat with Booker (4.8 from 4,905).

Is the Blue Cave worth it?

Yes on a sunny day around midday, when the light turns the water bright blue. Expect a short visit in a small boat and a queue in high summer.

Blue Lagoon or Blue Cave?

The Blue Lagoon is a half day of swimming near Split; the Blue Cave is a full 10-hour day further out that also covers Vis and Hvar.

Where do boat tours leave from in Split?

From the waterfront: the Riva, Obala Lazareta, Trumbićeva obala and Prokurative square.

When do Split boat tours run?

Roughly April to October, busiest June to early September.
