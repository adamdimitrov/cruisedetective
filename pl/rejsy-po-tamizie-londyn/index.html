Pomarańczowy= miejsca spotkań, kolorowe warstwy= trasy

Firmy oferujące rejsy po Tamizie zazwyczaj rozpoczynają rejs z Westminster Pier i oferują dwa punkty wyjścia na pokład: Tower Bridge Pier lub Greenwich. W zależności od rodzaju biletu masz możliwość pozostania na pokładzie i powrotu do punktu wyjścia.

Po drodze będziesz mieć okazję zwiedzić takie atrakcje jak:

  • Cutty Sark
  • Tower of London
  • St. Paul’s Cathedral
  • Tate Modern
  • Borough Market
  • South Bank Centre
  • Londyńskie Oko
  • Big Ben
  • Houses of Parliament
  • Westminster Bridge
  • Royal Festival Hall
  • Cleopatra's Needle
  • Somerset House
  • Millennium Footbridge
  • Shakespeare's Globe Monument
  • H.M.S. Belfast

🚢 The Ship

  • The ship you'll board is a double-decker vessel with a 200-person capacity, offering covered or open-air seating, a bar, AC/heating, and live commentary via loudspeakers.

🛋️ The Lower Deck

  • The bottom deck features chairs with tables next to the windows and a small bar. The front section also has standalone chairs, but the relatively small windows do not offer the best views.

☀️ The Top Deck

  • The upper deck offers 360° panoramic views, with comfortable seating. The deck has both covered indoor and outdoor seats. The seats are oriented towards the front.

📌 Drinks and Snacks

  • The ship features a bar section where you can purchase drinks and snacks.

🗼 Cruise Itinerary

  • Starting from Westminster Pier, this cruise allows passengers to disembark at either Tower Bridge Pier or Greenwich Pier.
