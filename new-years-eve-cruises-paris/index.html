The 5 Best New Year's Eve Cruises in Paris (2026/27)

By Adam Dimitrov, Owner & Head Cruise Detective
Programs & prices from operators and booking platforms • Independent comparison
Last updated: Nov 10, 2026

Want to ring in 2027 on the Seine, with the Eiffel Tower sparkling at midnight? New Year's Eve is the most expensive night of the year on the river, and the best boats sell out by mid-December.

I compared the New Year's Eve cruises you can book online this year, from a €95 champagne cruise to a €514 gala dinner at the Eiffel Tower, and checked the detail most listings hide: most dinner boats are already back at the quay before midnight. Only a few are actually on the water when the clock strikes twelve.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Bateaux Parisiens is the only big operator with two very different New Year's Eve programs, and both are actually on the Seine around midnight, starting and ending at the foot of the Eiffel Tower.

The Gala Dinner (€514) sails from 21:00 to 00:30 with a 6-course menu (lobster medallions, South-West foie gras, veal with black truffle, cheese and a Maison Lenôtre dessert), two glasses of Pommery Champagne and a live orchestra, then dancing on board until 04:00. The Champagne Cruise (€95) is the budget way to see midnight from the water: 22:40 to 00:00 with a Pommery Pop, a salmon bun, a foie gras brioche and macarons.

💡 Tip: Paris closes roads around the Champs-Élysées and the Eiffel Tower on New Year's Eve and the métro gets packed after midnight. Reach your pier at least 30 minutes early and plan your way home (night buses, taxis, or walking) in advance.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

If being on the river when the clock strikes twelve is what matters, this is one of the few dinner cruises that does it: boarding from 20:30 at Port de Bercy, departure 21:30 and back only around 00:30.

You get Champagne and amuse-bouches, a special New Year's menu, a bottle of wine for two, a live singer during dinner and a DJ for midnight, plus individual tables, big bay windows and private parking at the pier.

💡 Tip: Paris closes roads around the Champs-Élysées and the Eiffel Tower on New Year's Eve and the métro gets packed after midnight. Reach your pier at least 30 minutes early and plan your way home (night buses, taxis, or walking) in advance.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Be aware: the boat is back at the quay at 23:15. Midnight happens at the dock, where the boat turns into a dance floor until 02:00.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

The early New Year's Eve service on Le Diamant Bleu runs from 18:00 to 20:00. You get a 3-course dinner (peppered foie gras with kumquat chutney, veal tenderloin with truffle and morel sauce, the chef's dessert), a welcome cocktail, wine and a singer-guitarist duo, all for €207 on GetYourGuide.

You're back at the quay by 20:00, so treat it as a proper dinner before your own countdown at a party, the Trocadéro or the Champs-Élysées. The boat has a 450 m² panoramic room and a 350 m² rooftop.

💡 Tip: Paris closes roads around the Champs-Élysées and the Eiffel Tower on New Year's Eve and the métro gets packed after midnight. Reach your pier at least 30 minutes early and plan your way home (night buses, taxis, or walking) in advance.

💡 Tip: Paris closes roads around the Champs-Élysées and the Eiffel Tower on New Year's Eve and the métro gets packed after midnight. Reach your pier at least 30 minutes early and plan your way home (night buses, taxis, or walking) in advance.
