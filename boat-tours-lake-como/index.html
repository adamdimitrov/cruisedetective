2026 Lake Como Boat Tour Comparison

The 9 Best Lake Como Boat Tours (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Owner & Head Cruise Detective
Independent side-by-side comparison
Last updated: Nov 19, 2026

The right Lake Como boat tour is the one that fits your day: an hour past the villas from Como town, a half day with a stop in Bellagio or Varenna, a classic wooden boat with a glass of Prosecco, or a boat of your own. Watch the small print on private boats: most add €50 an hour for fuel, paid on board, so a €180 boat is really €230 for the first hour. This page gets you to that boat in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared the Lake Como boat tours you can book online and kept the 9 that each do one job best, on what decides it: where it starts (Como or Varenna), the boat, the group size, whether you stop in Bellagio or Varenna, what is included and the real total with fuel, checked on each seller's own page. Is a Lake Como Boat Tour Worth It?

Yes. Most of Lake Como's famous villas face the water and are hidden behind walls on the road, so the lake is the only way to see them properly. A shared tour with a captain costs €46 to €63 for an hour; the public ferry is cheaper but has no commentary and doesn't slow down for the villas.

First decide where you are staying: from Como town you see the southern villas (Villa d'Este, Laglio, Nesso) and need 3 to 4 hours to reach Bellagio; from Varenna or Bellagio, the central lake's Villa del Balbianello and Villa Carlotta are minutes away. Best Time for a Lake Como Boat Tour

May to September is the main season: swim stops, long evenings and every operator running. Book sunset departures early in July and August.

From November to March the lake is quiet and often sunny but cold on the water, so choose a covered boat (#7, or the private boats with weather covers). Weekends are busiest at the Como jetties at any time of year.

Lake Como Boat Tours: FAQ

How much does a Lake Como boat tour cost?

Shared tours cost €25 to €63 per person for 1 to 2 hours 40, and €119 for a half day with stops in Bellagio and Varenna. Private boats start at €180 plus €50 an hour for fuel.

What is the best Lake Como boat tour?

From Como, Captain Mario's small-group tour (4.8, Prosecco included, from €49). For a wooden boat, the Venetian taxi (#2) or the 8-seat classic (#3). From Varenna, the €25 tour with a stop in Bellagio.

Do Lake Como boat tours stop in Bellagio?

Some do: Captain Mario's and The View's 4-hour options, the half-day Bellagio and Varenna tour, and the Varenna tour with 50 minutes in Bellagio.

Do private boats on Lake Como charge for fuel?

Most do: the two private tours here add €50 an hour for fuel, paid on board.

Can you see George Clooney's villa from a boat?

Yes, his summer home in Laglio is on the route of the longer tours from Como, including the half-day Bellagio and Varenna tour.

Do Lake Como boat tours run in winter?

Yes, on fewer departures. Pick a covered boat like #7 for November to March.
