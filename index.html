EDITORIAL ETHICS & METHODOLOGY

Why The Cruise Detective Exists

Vacations don't have a return policy. When you spend €300 on an anniversary dinner cruise, you cannot buy back your evening.

100% Anonymous Testing

We never contact boat operators beforehand or request press comps. We book with personal credit cards, stand in standard boarding lines, and eat the exact food served to paying travelers.

Candid Course & Table Audits

If a sea bream arrives lukewarm or a table is wedged against an engine bulkhead, we state it plainly. We audit course preparation quality, wine pours, and acoustic piano volumes without compromise.

Zero Pay-to-Play Rankings

No operator can buy rank #1 or sponsor a favorable review. All affiliate links use transparent partner tracking that matches or beats direct dock prices.
