2026 Spree Cruise Comparison

The 10 Best Berlin Boat Tours (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Owner & Head Cruise Detective
Compared side by side on live listings • Independent comparison
Last updated: Nov 15, 2026

Nearly every 1-hour Spree cruise runs the same loop past the Reichstag and Museum Island for €17 to €25, so the real choice is the boat, the guide and the pier: here are the 10 worth booking, from a €6-per-hour lake trip to a 4-course dinner cruise.

Quick picks

  • Best overall: Hadynski: Glass-Roof Boat, Live Guide, glass-roof boat with a live guide, €18
  • Best long tour: Stern und Kreis: Bridges Tour, Spree + Landwehr Canal in 3.25 hours
  • Best evening: FLAGSHIP.BERLIN: Evening Yacht, yacht with a glass of Crémant
  • Best value: Stern und Kreis: Müggelsee, 3.5 hours to Müggelsee, €6.30 an hour

Summary & Review Overview ▾

If you only have an hour, this is the one. Reederei Hadynski runs two glass panorama boats, MS Carola and MS Franziska, and puts a live guide on every departure who switches between German and English. That is why it rates 4.7 from 7,465 reviews, the best score of any 1-hour Spree cruise.

The route is the classic central loop past Museum Island, the Reichstag and the government quarter. In summer the glass roof is fully open; from November it closes and the heating goes on, so it is also the best rainy-day pick. At €18.06 it costs the same as the audio-guide boats.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Berlin's 1-hour cruises all turn around at the same two points. The Brückenfahrt by Stern und Kreis is the one that keeps going: through the centre on the Spree, then back along the Landwehr Canal through Kreuzberg, under about 40 bridges, past the Technikmuseum and Potsdamer Platz.

It is the most-reviewed long cruise in Berlin, with 4.6 from more than 4,800 reviews, and at €32.90 for 3.25 hours it is the cheapest way to see the city from the water per hour. Bring a jacket for the open deck, and know that the ticket cannot be refunded.

Is a Berlin Boat Tour Worth It?

A Spree cruise covers Museum Island, the Berlin Cathedral, the Reichstag, the Chancellery and the Hauptbahnhof, sights that take half a day on foot, in one hour and for about €18. That is the best-value hour in the city for a first visit, especially in the cold months on a heated boat.

The catch: almost every 1-hour cruise runs the same loop: it turns at the Mühlendamm lock in the east and the Haus der Kulturen der Welt in the west. So don't choose by route. Choose by guide (live on #1, 12 audio languages on #3), by boat (the 1908 Hemingway at #4) and by pier. If you want more than the government quarter, the longer trips are cheaper per hour: €10 an hour on the bridges tour and €6.30 an hour to Müggelsee.

Best Time for a Spree Cruise

Late afternoon into dusk. The government quarter and Museum Island look best in low light, and from November to February sunset is around 16:00, so a 15:30 departure catches it. In summer, the evening yacht (#5) and the dinner cruise (#10) are timed for the long light.

In winter, pick a heated boat with a roof. Reederei Hadynski's glass-roof boats sail daily until the end of December. The lake cruises from Wannsee and Treptow are at their best from spring to autumn, when the beaches, gardens and villas are green; check the booking calendar for your date.

Berlin Boat Tours: FAQ

How much does a boat tour in Berlin cost?

A 1-hour Spree cruise through the centre costs €17 to €25 per adult. Longer trips cost €19 to €33 for 1.75 to 3.5 hours, an evening yacht with a glass of Crémant is €39, and a dinner cruise starts at €79.90.

Which Berlin boat tour has a live guide in English?

Reederei Hadynski's 1-hour glass-roof cruise has a live guide in German and English on every departure, and it is the best-rated 1-hour cruise at 4.7 from 7,465 reviews. Most other boats use recorded commentary or an audio guide.

Where do Berlin boat tours leave from?

The 1-hour cruises leave from piers around Museum Island and Friedrichstraße: Hackescher Markt, the Nikolaiviertel, Reichstagufer and the FLAGSHIP.BERLIN pier below the Reichstag. Lake cruises start at Wannsee (S-Bahn), the Müggelsee trip at Treptower Park, and the dinner cruise in Charlottenburg.

Do Berlin boat tours run in winter?

Yes. Heated boats like Reederei Hadynski's glass-roof cruise sail daily from March to December, and November and December departures often serve mulled wine at the bar. For the lake trips, check the booking calendar for your date.

Is a Berlin boat tour worth it?

Yes, if you pick the right length. One hour on the Spree shows Museum Island, the Reichstag and the government quarter without walking, for about €18. With an afternoon free, the 3.25-hour bridges tour adds Kreuzberg and the Landwehr Canal for about €10 per hour.

Can I cancel a Berlin boat tour for free?

Six of the 10 cruises here can be cancelled for free up to 24 hours before: the Hadynski, Stern und Kreis 1-hour, Hemingway, evening yacht, Potsdam and Müggelsee tickets. The bridges tour, Seven Lakes, East Side and dinner cruise tickets are non-refundable.
