23|How much does a Vistula river cruise in Warsaw cost?
24|Shared cruises cost €15.89 to €31.77 per person for 30 to 60 minutes. The best value is the Krypa galar at €17.06 for 54 minutes. A whole private boat for up to 12 starts at €211.05, and the 3-hour wildlife safari from Modlin is €113.47 per person.
25|Do Vistula cruises run in winter?
26|Some do. The Krypa galars (heated), the iBarka (glazed and heated, with a toilet), Flota 511's covered boat and the Warsaw Boats private boat are bookable every day in November and December. Kurka Wodna sails on selected dates and Miami Wars on weekends. The AQUATICA gondola and Rejsowisko restart in March and April.
27|Where do Warsaw river cruises leave from?
28|Most leave from the left-bank boulevards in Powiśle, below the Copernicus Science Centre (metro M2: Centrum Nauki Kopernik): the Marina Warszawa barge, the Dworzec Wodny barges and Solec 8. Kurka Wodna is a little south on Bulwar Flotylli Wiślanej, Rejsowisko is at Poniatowski Bridge, and the wildlife safari starts in Modlin, outside the city.
29|Which Warsaw cruise includes a drink?
30|Warsaw by BOAT's evening galar includes a welcome drink (alcohol-free prosecco in summer, mulled wine in winter), and Kurka Wodna includes coffee, tea, soft drinks and snacks. Krypa sells an evening version with one drink from €27.30, and the iBarka has a bar on board.
31|Is there an English-speaking guide on Vistula cruises?
32|Flota 511 has an English-speaking skipper, and Warsaw by BOAT's skippers tell stories about the city. Krypa and AQUATICA use an audio guide on your own phone, so bring headphones. The wildlife safari guide speaks Polish.
33|Can I bring my own drinks on a Vistula boat?
34|Yes on Warsaw by BOAT's galar and on Rejsowisko's motorboat, where eating and drinking on board is allowed. On the private galar, bringing your own drinks costs a PLN 100 corkage fee, while Kurka Wodna's private catamaran allows your own food and drinks at no extra charge.

571|Best Time for a Vistula Cruise
572|May to September is the main season.
573|Every operator sails, the beaches on the right bank are busy, and the sun sets late (around 9pm in June), so an evening cruise ends in daylight. Book the last departure before sunset for the Old Town lit up on the way back.
574|October to March is a different list.
575|In mid-November only the Krypa galars, the iBarka, Flota 511, the Warsaw Boats private boat and the wildlife safari are bookable every day, Kurka Wodna and Miami Wars sail on some days, and Warsaw by BOAT stops after 6 December. Sunset comes around 3:30pm in December, so even an afternoon cruise is a night cruise. Pick a heated or covered boat: the
576|iBarka
577|or the
578|Krypa galars
579|Watch the water level in late summer.
580|In dry spells the Vistula drops and some boats change route (Kurka Wodna heads south to Siekierki instead of the Old Town). Flota 511's shallow-draft boat is built for it.
