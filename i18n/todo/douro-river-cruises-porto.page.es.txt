33|How much does a Douro river cruise in Porto cost?
34|A 50-minute six-bridges cruise costs €18 to €20. Two-hour small-boat and sunset cruises cost €31 to €60 and usually include a drink, and a full day upriver to Régua with breakfast and lunch costs €76.
35|What are the six bridges of Porto?
36|From the river mouth upstream: Arrábida, Dom Luís I, Infante D. Henrique, Maria Pia, São João and Freixo. Every cruise on this list passes all six; the 2-hour boats also sail towards Foz, where the Douro meets the Atlantic.

43|The city cruises run all year, and most boats are covered or carry blankets, so light rain is fine. Operators cancel for storms and offer a new date or a refund. The full-day Douro Valley cruise does not sail in winter.

463|Is a Douro River Cruise in Porto Worth It?
464|Yes, and it is one of the cheapest good things to do in Porto. The city is built on two steep banks, so the river is the only place where you see Ribeira, the Gaia Port cellars and all six bridges at once.
465|The real choice is between two kinds of boat. The 50-minute six-bridges cruises cost €18 to €20, leave from the old town and are mostly about the view. The 2-hour boats cost €31 to €60, leave from the marinas, sail out to the river mouth and usually include wine or Port. If you have a spare evening, the 2-hour boat is the better deal per hour.

498|Best Time for a Douro Cruise
499|Book the last departure of the day. Porto faces west down the river, so the sunset over the river mouth is the best light you will get, from around 17:15 in December to around 21:00 in late June.
500|From May to September the sunset boats sell out first, so book a few days ahead. In winter the city cruises still run, but take a jacket: the 2-hour boats reach open water at Foz, where it is always windier than in Ribeira. The Douro Valley day cruise only sails from spring to autumn.
