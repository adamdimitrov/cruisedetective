## Before you book

- **Piers**: most boats leave from the Eiffel Tower piers (Port de la Bourdonnais, Port de Suffren). Bateaux Mouches is at Pont de l'Alma, about 15 minutes on foot.
- **Eiffel Tower sparkle**: after dark the tower sparkles for 5 minutes at the start of every hour. Time a night sailing to pass it then.
- **Rain**: every large boat has a heated, enclosed cabin, so you only lose the open deck. The private boat (#7) is the exception.
- **Open-dated tickets**: Bateaux Parisiens and Bateaux Mouches tickets work on any sailing that day.
