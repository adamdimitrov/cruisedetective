Are Paris Dinner Cruises Worth It?

Yes, absolutely!

Don’t listen to those who say the food on dinner cruises in Paris is not gourmet enough or that you cannot enjoy the sightseeing while having dinner.

The food is generally excellent, and going on a dinner cruise at least once is a must.

Of course, not all dinner cruises are the same, but you can find the perfect one for you below.

Paris Dinner Cruise Itinerary

Orange = Meeting Points, Colored Layers = Itineraries

Every dinner cruise in Paris follows the same itinerary, which runs between the Statue of Liberty and Pont de Sully. The route passes nearly 20 landmarks, including:

  • The Eiffel Tower
  • Pont Alexandre III
  • Musée d'Orsay
  • Grand Palais
  • Petit Palais
  • Institut de France
  • Île de la Cité
  • Notre Dame Cathedral
  • Hotel de Ville
  • Louvre Museum
  • Place de la Concorde
  • The Statue of Liberty

🚢 The Ship 🚢

  • The ship is flawless in every aspect - huge (500-person capacity), elegant, luxurious, spacious, and high-end. I felt excited on this cruise, which is a big thing after trying out four other dinner cruises.
  • Note that Bateaux Mouches has 5 dinner cruise boats in its fleet, so you might get a slightly different ship than I did.
  • The ship features large windows that offer magnificent views of the Parisian cityscape.
  • The interior is tastefully decorated and has a lot of space.

☀️ Rooftop Deck

  • The ship has a massive rooftop terrace where you can ascend any time you want to enjoy the view and snap some photos.

📌 Vibe ✨

  • Sophisticated, romantic, and high-end — they nailed the perfect energy and feel of the evening.

🎙️ Live Music 🎵

  • There is a live piano and violin duo playing on the boat, which adds to the "Romantic Parisian dinner on the Seine" ambiance without being overwhelming, like on Le Diamant Bleu.

📌 Staff & Service 🧑

  • The staff is unusually friendly and chatty, not to mention fast, making you feel like a cherished guest. This is rare in Europe, like a white raven, so when you experience it, it leaves a lasting impression.

📌 Seating 🪑

  • Seat reservations are not available. In other words, seats are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis. Choose the Excellence ticket to have window seating priority.
  • If the ship is not at full capacity, all passengers will receive a window seat (subject to availability).

📌 Tickets & Pricing Tiers 🎟️

  • There are two ticket tiers: Prestige and Excellence.
  • Prestige tier (130€): 3-to-4 courses, gourmet food, full bottle of wine, 1L bottle of Evian, coffee.
  • Excellence tier (155€): 4 courses, higher quality dishes (foie gras, Châteaubriand), 24mo Comté, Moët & Chandon Champagne, and window seating priority.
  • The price is middle-of-the-road in the dinner cruise category, and you get the most bang for your buck out of all the cruises I tested.

📌 Verdict — Bateaux Mouches or Parisiens?

Both operators deliver exceptional Seine dinner cruises with high review volumes.

However, Bateaux Mouches offers significantly better value: more spacious open-air rooftop access, larger portions, friendlier service, and a full bottle of wine per 2 persons (versus per 4 on Parisiens).

Parisiens remains a solid second place for travelers prioritizing an Eiffel Tower departure pier.

Are Paris Dinner Cruises a Lame Tourist Traps?

While dinner cruises are popular with tourists, I wouldn't call them a "trap", which means someone is overcharging you for a mediocre experience.

By definition, if you pay a reasonable price for a fabulous experience, you got you're money's worth. In this case, just choose Bateaux Mouches, and you'll be all right.

I think the concept of dinner cruises on the Seine is great.

But yes, some of them aren't worth it - there's a significant gap in execution. Just read my article and you'll see the clear differences between the top and last items on this list.

Note: All cruises offer photography service, which is overpriced in my opinion (20 euros).

What to Wear for a Paris Dinner Cruise?

For virtually all dinner cruises, smart casual clothing does the job.‍

You go as high up in the formal department as you'd like, but never the opposite - don't go too casual or they might turn you away at the entrance. Too casual means shorts, casual t-shirts, flip flops, athletic wear, pyjamas, bathrobes and overly worn or ripped jeans.

The cruise staff still expect a certain level of refinement that matches the elegant atmosphere they're trying to create on board.

What Time of Year is Best for a Dinner Cruise?

Paris dinner cruises operate year-round, but each season offers different advantages:

  • Spring (April-June): Comfortable temperatures and longer daylight hours let you see more landmarks in natural light.
  • Summer (July-August): Warmest weather for open-air viewing, though these are the most crowded months.
  • Fall (September-October): Beautiful autumn colors along the riverbanks with fewer tourists.
  • Winter (November-March): Special charm with holiday lights and decorations, particularly in December.

Summer offers the latest sunset times, which means you might see both daylight and nighttime views during your cruise. However, shoulder seasons (spring and fall) often provide the best balance of good weather and smaller crowds.

Is There A Photo Service On Board?

  • Every cruise offers a professional photography service to capture the evening. Pricy, but could be worth it for high-quality family or couple photos as souvenirs. You can always inspect both the digital and physical photos before deciding to buy.
  • The photo service is most affordable at 15€ per photo on Bateaux Mouches compared to other cruises, which charge 20-25€ per photo.

Birthday Celebrations and Anniversaries 🎂

  • Every cruise I have tried has been accommodating for birthday celebrations. These take place by the end of the cruise, with a birthday musical cue, clapping, and a piece(s) of cake/dessert with firework candles.
  • Marriage proposals are also possible on certain cruises, just ask the company if they offer them
